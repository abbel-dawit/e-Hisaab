How it works

From sale to registered invoice and receipt in four steps.

  1. Register your business

    Enter your TIN and VAT number. We connect your branches to the Ministry's registration system.

  2. Create the invoice

    Pick the buyer, add items from your catalogue. VAT and withholding are calculated for you.

  3. The Ministry clears it

    The invoice is signed and sent. Seconds later it returns with its IRN, RRN and QR code.

  4. Get paid, receipt registered

    Record full or part payments by cash, bank, telebirr or CBE Birr. Each payment gets its own registered e-receipt, sent by SMS.

Questions

What businesses ask us first.

Who has to use electronic invoicing?

Directive No. 1142/2026 makes a compliant sales registration system mandatory for taxpayers who keep books of account, following a rollout schedule set by the Ministry of Revenues. VAT-registered large taxpayers are expected first.

Do payment receipts need to be registered too?

Yes. Under Directive No. 1142/2026 receipts are registered with the Ministry as well as invoices. e-Hisaab registers every payment as its own e-receipt with a Receipt Registration Number (RRN) and QR code, including part payments, and registers the cancellation if a receipt is voided.

What are the IRN, RRN and QR code?

When the Ministry's Electronic Invoice Registration System accepts an invoice it returns an Invoice Registration Number (IRN), a Receipt Registration Number (RRN) and a QR code. Buyers and auditors use them to verify the invoice is genuine.

What happens if the internet or power goes down?

You keep issuing invoices with a temporary QR code. e-Hisaab queues them and registers each one automatically once the connection returns. The directive allows 72 hours after restoration.

Do I still need a fiscal printer?

No. Invoices are created and registered digitally. You can still print on any ordinary printer if a buyer asks for paper.

Can it connect to our ERP or POS?

Yes. The Enterprise plan includes an API so your existing system can send invoices through e-Hisaab for registration.
